Our Story

A homestead
rooted in craft

We're the Gibson family — and this is our homestead in Lowell, Indiana. What started as a love for working with our hands has grown into something we're proud to share with our community.

We grow seasonal cut flowers using sustainable methods, bake slow-fermented sourdough from our own starter, and shape handmade pottery in our home studio under the name Birch Tree Designs.

At Gibson Ranch, everything is made the slow way — because we believe the best things come from patience, care, and a little dirt under your fingernails.
