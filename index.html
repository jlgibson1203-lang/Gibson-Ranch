Our Story

A homestead
rooted in craft

Gibson Ranch is a family homestead in Lowell, Indiana — a place where creativity meets the land. We grow seasonal cut flowers, bake sourdough bread from scratch, and shape handmade pottery through our studio brand, Birch Tree Designs.

Everything we make is rooted in the same philosophy: slow down, use your hands, and create something worth caring about.

Whether it's a weekly bouquet subscription, a rustic loaf of sourdough, or a one-of-a-kind mug from the kiln — we put intention into everything that leaves the ranch.
